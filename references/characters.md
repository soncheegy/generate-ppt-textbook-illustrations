## Multi-character rules

- Use every named anchor in the same generation call for a plot scene.
- State each identity separately and assign the requested action to the correct character.
- Preserve relative scale: 小括狐 is shorter than the two human children; the human children are similar height.
- Never merge features, swap outfits, duplicate a character, or introduce unnamed classmates.
